Living Room Theatre

Pick a movie from your own device, share a link, and friends watch it live in their browsers. No upload, no account.

Host a screening

Choose a video file of any size. It plays from your device and streams to your guests.

Have a party link?

Open the link your host sent you. It joins automatically.

In the room

The movie is streamed straight from the host's device, so the host must keep this tab open and use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a laptop or desktop. Pause, seek and speed on the host's player are mirrored to every guest. Each guest uses a share of the host's upload speed, so 4 to 6 guests is a good size.
